GETTING STARTED

How to use ShareSquad

Pick a topic for a full walkthrough, or use the short notes below.

Shared groups

Create a group, invite members, and split household or trip bills.

 Receipt scanning

Local OCR first, optional provider help, always review before saving.

 Clear balances

See who owes whom, record settlements, and download monthly reports.

Track your personal spending

Open Personal Tracker, choose Add Expense or Scan Receipt, review the details, choose a category, and save. Use the month selector to review totals and categories.

Split a bill with one friend

Open Friends and choose Split with someone. Select an existing group member for a direct split, or invite someone by email or private link. Each person has one combined card and monthly history.
