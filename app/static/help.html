GETTING STARTED

How to use ShareSquad

Pick a topic for a full walkthrough, or use the short notes below.

Shared groups

Create a group, invite members, and split household or trip bills.

 Receipt scanning

Local OCR first, optional provider help, always review before saving.

 Clear balances

Who pays whom, record payments on Friends, and download monthly reports.

Track your personal spending

Open Personal Tracker, choose Add Expense or Scan Receipt, review the details, choose a category, and save. Use the month selector to review totals and categories.

Split a bill with one friend

Open Friends and use New bill (or Add bill on a friend’s page). The same form supports an existing friend, email invite, or private link. Each person has one combined card. Record full or partial payments there — that is also where past payments appear.

Month and late bills

An expense belongs to the calendar month you save it. Older receipt dates saved this month still count toward this month’s balances and show under Added late in digests. Generating a PDF does not lock the month.

Try the demo

From the landing page, Try the full demo opens a private sandbox of the current app with sample roommate data. It matches a regular account; sign up when you want to keep your own groups.
