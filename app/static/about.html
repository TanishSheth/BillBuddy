ABOUT SHARE SQUAD

Shared money should be simple to understand.

ShareSquad is an expense-splitting and personal-spending app created by Tanish Sheth. It helps people record bills clearly, see who owes whom, and keep a useful history without losing the human context behind each expense.

Built for everyday sharing

Use a shared group for households, trips, events, and projects. Use Friends for private one-to-one bills and to record payments. Use the Personal Tracker for spending that belongs only to you. Bills count for the month you save them; digests never lock a month.

Clarity before automation

Receipt scanning suggests details, but you review the merchant, date, amount, category, payer, and split before anything is saved. The goal is a clear record everyone can understand.
