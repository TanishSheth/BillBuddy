CLEAR BALANCES

Know who owes whom—without the awkward math.

Every saved expense records who paid and each person’s share. ShareSquad totals those shares for the period so the group can see balances at a glance.

How balances are calculated

Open a shared group and check Balances for the current month. The view combines every expense in that period: people who paid more than their share are owed; people who paid less owe the difference.

Settle when money moves

When someone pays you back, the payer can mark the transfer paid and the recipient can confirm it. The recipient can also record a received payment directly. Confirmed payments stay in the history while the group’s remaining balance updates.

Reports and currency

Download a PDF report for the month when you need a shared record. Groups can use a settlement currency when reviewing balances, even if individual expenses were entered in other supported currencies.

Friends and personal spending

One-to-one friend splits have their own clear owed / owed-to-you card. Personal Tracker keeps your solo spending separate from shared group balances.
