SHARED GROUPS

Roommates, trips, and teams in one place.

A shared group is the space for expenses that more than two people need to track together—household bills, a vacation, a project, or a recurring team lunch.

Create a group

Sign in, choose New Group, name the group, and pick its default currency. That currency is used for balances and settlement in the group.

Invite members

Open Members to add an existing ShareSquad user by email, choose one from your Friends list, or share a group link. Members are added automatically and never need to accept an invitation.

Add and split expenses

Add a bill manually or scan a receipt. Choose who paid, choose who shares the expense, and pick equal, percentage, exact, or shares. Save when the details look right.

What you get

Monthly periods, clear balances, settlement records, and downloadable PDF reports so the group can see what happened and settle without awkward spreadsheets.
